Quick Reference Card 📋

Keep this open at the console during service.

🟢 Start of Sunday

  • Console & stage boxes on — wait to fully boot
  • Wireless receivers & computers on
  • Mains down, then speakers on LAST
  • Recall the Sunday scene
  • Line check every mic
  • Mute anything not used yet

🔑 The Four Buttons

  • SELECT — picks the channel to work on
  • MUTE lit (red) — channel is OFF, no sound
  • SOLO — listen in headphones only
  • Fader near 0 — adjust by ear during service
  • Wrong channels? Check the layer buttons
  • Monitors? Sends on Fader, then exit it

📊 Setting Gain

  • SELECT channel → press SOLO → put on headphones
  • Have them talk/sing at real service volume
  • Green + touch of yellow = just right ✅
  • Hitting red → turn gain DOWN
  • Turn on Low Cut for all voice mics
  • Fader near 0, adjust by ear

⚠️ Feedback or Problem

  • Pull down the RINGING mic's fader first
  • Mute unused open mics
  • Don't grab the main LR fader first
  • No sound → mic on? mute off? fader up? right layer?
  • Fader does nothing → wrong layer or Sends on Fader lit
  • Stay calm — mute it, then call for help

🔴 End of Sunday

  • Mute mics, bring main LR fader down
  • Speakers & amps off FIRST
  • Wireless off, batteries out or charging
  • Stop recording & remove USB drive
  • Console off LAST
  • Note any problems for sound lead

🚫 Never During Service

  • Don't save over the Sunday scene
  • Don't change routing or Setup screens
  • Don't change gain without sound lead OK
  • Don't leave Sends on Fader lit
  • Don't leave unused mics open
  • Livestream problem → tell sound lead, don't fix routing
